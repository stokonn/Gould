# User Guide — Gould

> **Releases:** [0.12 Pre-release](https://github.com/stokonn/Gould/releases/tag/v0.12) · [0.13 Project Renaissance (pre-release)](https://github.com/stokonn/Gould/releases/tag/v0.13). **Both releases are pre-releases; 0.13 is newer.**

The features below shipped with 0.12 Pre-release and remain in 0.13 Pre-release, which focuses on size, performance and recording recovery reliability.

## Library and Discover

Maintain your personal repertoire in Library and explore sourced music records in Discover. Both open canonical catalogue details. Saved local records and optional online provider search are distinct; online search requires Settings consent. Check Sources & credits for provenance and licence information.

## Studio and recordings

Studio offers Manual and experimental Smart Practice. Smart detection estimates active playing rather than perfectly identifying music. Starting audio recording is a separate explicit action. **Pause** freezes credited practice time, while a deliberately started recording continues until **Stop recording** or **Finish & Review**. Recovery of interrupted takes is explicit; the app does not automatically resume microphone capture.

The expandable metronome supports tempo and beats per bar. Pieces played manages the session queue, including reorder, remove and Undo; previous credited intervals do not change. Session and piece notes persist in the draft.

Use **Previous** for Practice History and saved Recordings. Play, seek and export saved WAV files; deletion requires confirmation. Keep interrupted original data until recovery or export is complete.

## Profile, widgets and backups

Profile includes local identity, practice statistics, badges and showcase. Quick Practice and Weekly Activity launcher widgets open Studio without starting timers or the microphone. JSON backup format 6 includes recording metadata; a **separate media ZIP** is needed to transfer audio bytes. Never treat a CSV or diagnostic recovery export as a portable backup.
